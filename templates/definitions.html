Core Governance & Boundary Rules
Treatment of Cancelled Orders

Orders with order_status = 'Cancelled' are strictly excluded from Gross Sales, Net Sales, Order Count, Discounts, and Returns. They represent aborted transactions that generated no fulfilled economic value. However, they are tracked in an operational risk KPI (Cancellation Rate).

Treatment of Discounts & Markdowns

Discounts represent recognized price concessions (vouchers, promotional campaign codes, item-level discounts). They are subtracted from Gross Sales to reach Net Sales. Discounts on cancelled orders are ignored.

Treatment of Merchandise Returns

Customer refunds on Delivered or Completed orders are captured in return_amount_optional and deducted from Gross Sales to establish Net Realized Revenue. Returns cannot occur on Cancelled orders.
